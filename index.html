    Ask the filings

    Every answer comes with a chart, the table behind it and the exact SQL.

    The data

    Every H-1B Labor Condition Application the Department of Labor published from FY2024 to FY2026 Q3, plus the OFLC 2026-27 prevailing wage tables. An LCA is the wage filing an employer makes before a petition. It is not an approval or a visa.

    The cleanup

    Built offline: wages converted to yearly amounts, an employer's many legal names merged into one, free-text job titles sorted into role families, every worksite mapped to its official wage area, and each offer's wage level and lottery entries computed.

    The agent

    Gemini writes DuckDB SQL over the cleaned tables, using lookup tools for employers, roles and places. Guardrails block known mistakes, and it says plainly when the data can't answer something.
    Sources: DOL OFLC LCA disclosure data · OFLC prevailing wage tables 2026-27
    Lottery entries follow the wage-weighted selection rule (Level I = 1 … Level IV = 4). Not legal advice.
