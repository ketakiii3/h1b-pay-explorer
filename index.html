How many H-1B lottery entries does a offer in get?

Click a box to change it. The answer comes straight from the wage table, with no model involved.

    Since 2026 the H-1B lottery is wage-weighted. An offer at Level I of the official wage table gets one entry, and an offer at Level IV gets four. Check an offer on the ticket, then ask the filings who sponsors your role, what they pay and at which level they file.

    Built on 1.5 million H-1B filings from 107,000 employers, 442,919 official wage thresholds and USCIS's approvals per employer. For students on F-1 or OPT and anyone weighing an H-1B offer.

    Ask the filings

    Every answer comes with a chart, the table behind it and the exact SQL.

    The data

    Every H-1B Labor Condition Application the Department of Labor published from FY2024 to FY2026 Q3, plus the OFLC 2026-27 prevailing wage tables and USCIS's petition approvals and denials per employer for FY2024 to FY2026. An LCA is the wage filing an employer makes before a petition. It is not an approval or a visa; an approved petition is not a visa either.

    The cleanup

    Built offline: wages converted to yearly amounts, an employer's many legal names merged into one, free-text job titles sorted into role families, every worksite mapped to its official wage area, and each offer's wage level and lottery entries computed.

    The agent

    Gemini writes DuckDB SQL over the cleaned tables, using lookup tools for employers, roles and places. Guardrails block known mistakes, and it says plainly when the data can't answer something.
    Sources: DOL OFLC LCA disclosure data, OFLC prevailing wage tables 2026-27, USCIS H-1B Employer Data Hub
    Lottery entries follow the wage-weighted selection rule (Level I gets 1, Level IV gets 4). Not legal advice.
